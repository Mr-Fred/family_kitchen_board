# SavorAI — Family Kitchen Board & Smart Pantry Co-Pilot

100% Native Android, offline-first culinary assistant, shelf-life & two-tier pantry ledger, clean cookbook reader, hands-free step-by-step cook mode, and 21-slot family meal planner targeting **Android 12+ (`minSdk = 31`, `targetSdk = 34`) phones and tablets** universally—built with **Kotlin 2.2**, **Jetpack Compose (Material 3)**, **LiteRT-LM on-device multimodal AI**, and opt-in **Keystore-encrypted HTTPS Cloud LLM routing** (`applicationId = "com.familykitchen.board"`).

Calibrated out of the box for a **Family of 4** (`Fred`, `Wife`, `8-year-old`, and `2-year-old`) shopping in **Antipolo & Marikina, PH (`PHP`, `₱`)**, with built-in consumer household templates (`Solo Cook`, `Active Couple`, `Young Family + Baby`, `Family of 4`, `Large / Multi-Gen 6`) and multi-currency formatting (`PHP`, `USD`, `EUR`, `GBP`, `XOF`).

## Highlights & Design System (`SavorAI`)

- **Dual-Mode AI Architecture (100% Offline-First Default + 1-Tap Gemma 4 Download + Opt-In Encrypted Cloud API)**:
  - **On-Device Offline Default (`AiExecutionMode.ON_DEVICE`)**: Runs 100% locally via `LiteRtMultimodalEngine` across **Android 12+ phones and tablets** with automatic `ActivityManager` RAM-tier profile selection (`Compact 1B (6–8 GB RAM)`, `Balanced 2B (8–12 GB RAM)`, `Gemma 4 Vision (8 GB+ RAM)`), optional mobile `libOpenCL.so` GPU acceleration (`android:required="false"`) with automatic CPU fallback, compact `360dp` `1.35x` font-scale layout resilience, bundled fonts/artwork, bilingual speech sessions (`en-US` & `fr-FR`), **zero** `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` or `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` permissions, and an approval-gated resumable Gemma 4 (`E2B` ~2.0 GB / `E4B` ~3.2 GB) `.litertlm` downloader (`ModelDownloadApprovalDialog.kt` + `OnDeviceModelDownloader.kt`, checking `StatFs` free space and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` Wi-Fi vs metered cellular status before streaming into private `filesDir/models/<name>.part` with `HTTP 206` `Range` resumption).
  - **Opt-In Cloud API Key Mode (`AiExecutionMode.CLOUD_API`)**: Encrypts user-supplied credentials at rest in `KeystoreCloudCredentialVault` (`AndroidKeyStore` `AES-256-GCM`), enforces HTTPS-only `networkSecurityConfig` (`cleartextTrafficPermitted="false"`, `android:allowBackup="false"`, strict `ALLOWED_CLOUD_HOSTS` SSRF allowlist), and routes text + multimodal vision with header-only authentication across **Google Gemini** (`gemini-3.8-flash`, `gemini-4-*`), **OpenAI** (`gpt-6.2-sol`, `gpt-6.2-astra`), **Anthropic Claude** (`claude-sonnet-5-5`, `claude-opus-5-5`), and **OpenRouter** (`google/gemini-4-neon`), complete with 1-tap **`Sync Live Models`** (`GET /models` discovery) and automatic legacy model migration.
- **4-Step First-Run Onboarding Experience (`ui/onboarding/`)**: Launches automatically on brand-new cold installs (`hasCompletedOnboarding == false`) to tailor household template, cook names & **Preferred Family Cuisines** (`French` & `Yoruba and West African` default, plus `Filipino`, `Mediterranean`, `East Asian`, `Pan-African`, `Global Comfort`, and custom tags), local currency & neighborhood market (`PHP` `₱` default + `USD`, `EUR`, `GBP`, `XOF`), starting pantry stock (`Quick-Check Kitchen Basics` 16-staple checklist, `Empty Pantry` `0`-slot `Blank Week`, or 44-item `Sample Kitchen`), and optional 1-tap On-Device Gemma 4 `.litertlm` download / SAF import / Cloud API key setup—committing in a single `O(1)` atomic batch write with a 1-tap `Skip · Demo` escape hatch and anytime replay from `Family Profile` or `QA Lab`.
- **Artisanal Material 3 Design System, Clean UI Copy & Auto-Dismissing Notifications ([`.agents/DESIGN.md`](.agents/DESIGN.md) + [`docs/ui-spec/`](docs/ui-spec/))**: Pairs bundled offline `Newsreader` editorial serif headlines and tabular `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`tnum`) metrics with Warm Daylight (`#FBFBF9` Linen, `#2E7D5A` Herbal Sage, `#E06D53` Terracotta Clay, `#F48C06` Warm Apricot) and Ambient Counter Dark OLED palettes, clean punctuation-free UI labels (`0` slashes `/` or parentheses `()` across tabs, chips, buttons, and switchers), a bespoke `108dp` adaptive Dutch-oven cocotte launcher icon + matching Compose `Canvas` brand emblem (`SavorBrandLogo.kt`), and smooth auto-dismissing notifications (`4s` confirmations, `6s` error/long alerts, `5s` permission notices, and `8s` `Undo` bar).
- **4-Tab High-Craft Adaptive Shell + Contextual Sous-Chef & Mini-Timer Overlays (`KitchenScaffold`)**:
  - **`Plan` (`TodayAndWeekScreen`)**: Real civil-calendar Meal Plan (`Mon 23`..`Sun 29` date chips, `Nov 23 – Nov 29` header, `This Week` & `Next Week` horizon toggle, `FixedKitchenClock` / `SystemKitchenClockAdapter` wall-clock sync), `Start New Week` rollover (`Full Week`, `Breakfast and Dinner`, `Dinners Only`, `Blank Week` with uncooked carry-over & `isPinned` anchor retention), `Saved Menus` (`French and Yoruba Family Week` ranked `#1`), slot move/swap (`EC-01`), explicit skip reasons (`Ate Leftovers`, `Eating Out`, `Skipped`), 8:30 PM night-prep banner, `EC-08` child safety callouts, and 4-station **Batch Prep**.
  - **`Pantry` (`SmartPantryScreen`)**: Stocked-only `Fridge`, `Dry Pantry`, and `Freezer` shelves (isolating depleted `0g` rows in `Out of Stock` so empty kitchens never show phantom expiry badges), live `expiryDateIso` calendar aging, `Action Required` 48h rescue cards in `PHP` (`₱`) with 1-tap **`Freeze to Rescue`** (`+90d`/`+180d`) and **`Used`**, in-place **`Quick Shelf Check`** (`Empty · Low · Half · Full` proportional pills), global ingredient renaming, and `Copy Pantry` + `Save PDF` A4 inventory export.
  - **`Recipes` (`CookbookGridScreen` + `CleanRecipeReaderScreen`)**: 2-column visual Cookbook Grid across **42 household recipes** (`30` seed + `12` curated Yoruba, French Bistro, & Filipino heritage classics) with `For Our Family`, `In Stock`, `Under 25m`, & `Saved` filters; Clean Recipe Reader with `All Recipes` back navigation, `Make Our Version` recipe forking, custom `Edit` & `Delete`, macro-recomputing ingredient swaps, and `1x | 2x | 4x` yield scaling; plus the **3-Mode Recipe Studio** (`Smart Paste` with `0` phantom stock, `Scan Cookbook` Gemma Vision OCR, and `Quick Editor`).
  - **`Shop` (`SmartGroceryScreen` + `UnpackGroceriesSheet`)**: Multi-market Smart Grocery List with **`All · Palengke · Supermarket`** venue pills, transparent **`Pantry Adjusted`** skipped-staples banner with 1-tap **`Ran Low`** requeue, editable buy quantities & prorated `₱` pricing, zero-boomerang **`Uncheck`**, `Copy List` + `Save PDF` A4 export, and 1-tap **`Unpack Groceries`** (`commitGroceryUnpackBatch` applying `Fridge · Pantry · Freezer` zone overrides and promoting food custom notes into `Pantry` in `1` `O(1)` write).
  - **Contextual `Ask Chef` (`SousChefModalSheet`) & Minimizable `Step-by-Step Cook Mode` (`ActiveCookMiniBar` + `PostCookCompletionSheet`)**: Persistent top-bar `Ask Chef` button opening the Single-Gemma Elephant & Goldfish + ADK Sous-Chef modal across all 4 tabs; full-screen wake-locked `StepByStepCookModeScreen` with `Minimize` into `ActiveCookMiniBar` (keeping countdown timers ticking live above the 4-tab bar), pantry-aware step Q&A, and post-cook `0`-BOM tomorrow **Leftover Lunch** scheduling + family reaction chips distilled into `HouseholdElephantMemory`.
