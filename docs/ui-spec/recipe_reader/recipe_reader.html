Pan-Seared Tuscan Salmon with Creamy Garlic Lemon Spinach

Method & Directions

4 Steps
Step 1 • Preparation restaurant

Pat salmon fillets completely dry with paper towels. Season both sides generously with flaky kosher salt and freshly cracked black pepper. Dry skin guarantees a golden, crackling sear.

Step 2 • The Sear Skin crisping

Heat olive oil in a heavy stainless steel or cast iron skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering. Add salmon skin-side down; press lightly with a spatula for 10 seconds, then cook undisturbed until 75% cooked through.

Do not move fish while searing
Step 3 • Flip & Rest swap_horiz

Flip fillets gently using a fish spatula and cook for an additional 3 minutes for tender medium doneness. Transfer to a warm plate to rest while preparing the pan sauce.

Step 4 • Creamy Pan Sauce soup_kitchen

Lower heat to medium. In the same pan, sauté minced garlic until aromatic (about 30 seconds). Fold in baby spinach in batches until wilted, then pour in heavy cream and lemon juice. Simmer for 2 minutes until glossy. Stir in parmesan and nestle salmon back in.
